Solve My Homework AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, disclosed, and protected when you use the Solve My Homework AI mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App. Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in the Terms of Service.

  1. SCOPE OF THIS POLICY

1.1 This Policy applies to information processed through the App on iOS and Android, and to communications you send to us. 1.2 This Policy does not apply to the practices of third parties we do not control, including the Apple App Store, Google Play, or the third-party processing services described in Section 5, each of which maintains its own privacy policy. 1.3 We may update this Policy from time to time as described in Section 13.

  1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

2.1 Anonymous device identifier. When the App is first launched, it generates a random identifier that is not derived from your name, email address, phone number, or hardware serial number. This identifier is used to enforce usage limits, verify PRO access, associate an optional account with this device, operate referrals, remember notification permission, measure aggregate feature use, prevent abuse, and diagnose reliability problems. It is distinct from the mobile advertising identifier described in Section 2.6. 2.2 Content you submit. When you type a question, submit a writing request, or photograph material, the text or image you submit is transmitted over an encrypted connection to our server and to the third-party processors described in Section 5 for the purpose of generating a response. If you sign in, limited history and answers you choose to save may also be stored as described in Sections 2.9 and 7.1. 2.3 Subscription information. If you purchase a PRO subscription, the transaction is processed entirely by Apple or Google. We receive only an anonymized entitlement status through our subscription management provider. We never receive your payment card number, billing address, or other payment credentials. 2.4 Support communications. If you contact us by email or through the in-App contact or feedback forms, we receive the information you choose to include. 2.5 Information we do NOT collect automatically. The App works without an account. If you do not sign in or contact us, we do not ask for your name, email address, physical address, phone number, contact list, precise geolocation, browsing history, or biometric data. If you choose Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google, the account information listed in Section 2.9 is collected. We do not use submitted Content for advertising or build advertising profiles about you. 2.6 Advertising data (free tier). The free version displays non-personalized advertisements served through Google AdMob. Google may process limited technical information for delivery, frequency capping, reporting, and fraud prevention. The App does not track you across other apps or websites and does not request permission to do so. PRO subscribers do not see advertisements. See Sections 5 and 12.

2.9 Optional account information. The App works fully without an account. If you choose to create one, we collect and store the following:

  • Email address — from Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google. Used to identify your account. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we only ever receive Apple's private relay address.
  • Display name — only if Apple or Google provides one. It is optional and used solely to greet you in the App.
  • Grade or study level — only if you choose to set it. Used to pitch explanations at the right level.
  • Your history and saved answers — so they appear on every device you sign in on and survive reinstalling the App.

2.10 Signing in. The App offers Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google only. Both are handled by Apple and Google themselves; we never see or store a password.

2.11 We do not sell, rent, or share account information with advertisers. Creating an account does not change the advertising you see: all advertising in the App is non-personalised for every user, signed in or not.

2.12 Deleting your account. You can delete your account and everything in it at any time from Settings → My Account → Delete my account, without contacting us. Deletion is immediate and permanent, and removes your email address, name, profile, history, and saved answers from our servers. Your app-store subscription is billed separately by Apple or Google and is not cancelled by deleting your account — cancel that through the store.

Deleting your account
You can delete your account and all of its data from inside the app, or from this page if you no longer have the app installed: How to delete your account and your data.

2.13 Interface translation. If you select a language the App does not ship with, the English wording of the App's own buttons and labels is sent to our AI provider to be translated, and the translated wording is cached on our server so it does not need translating again. This contains no personal information — only the App's own interface text.

  1. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

3.1 We use the information described above to: (a) generate the answers, explanations, translations, writing assistance, and calculations you request; (b) enforce free-tier daily limits; (c) verify and honor PRO subscription entitlements; (d) maintain the security, stability, and performance of the App and our server infrastructure; (e) respond to support inquiries; (f) display non-personalized advertisements to free-tier users through Google AdMob, as described in Section 2.6; and (g) comply with applicable legal obligations. 3.2 We do NOT use the Content you submit — your questions, images, or text — to build advertising profiles or to target advertising, and we do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes. Because all advertising in the App is non-personalized, advertisements are never selected using your Content, your interests, or your activity in other apps. 3.3 We do not use your Content to train our own artificial intelligence models. Third-party processors handle data in accordance with their own published policies, as described in Section 5.

  1. LEGAL BASES FOR PROCESSING (EEA/UK USERS)

Where the General Data Protection Regulation or the UK GDPR applies, we process information on the following legal bases: (a) performance of a contract, in order to provide the App and generate responses you request; (b) legitimate interests, in order to secure and operate the service, prevent abuse of free-tier limits, and improve reliability, balanced against your rights and freedoms; (c) consent, which we gather through a Google-certified consent message before serving advertisements to users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, and which you may withdraw at any time through the privacy options offered in that message; and (d) compliance with legal obligations, where processing is necessary to comply with applicable law.

  1. THIRD-PARTY PROCESSORS AND DISCLOSURES

5.1 We share information only as necessary to operate the App, with the following categories of processors: (a) AI processing providers (currently OpenAI), which receive the text of your questions and, where applicable, images you submit, in order to generate responses; (b) optical character recognition providers (currently Google Cloud Vision), which may receive images you submit in order to extract text, where that feature is enabled; (c) subscription management providers (currently RevenueCat), which process anonymized subscription entitlement data; (d) hosting and infrastructure providers, which process data incidentally in the course of operating our servers; and (e) advertising providers (currently Google AdMob), which serve non-personalized advertisements to free-tier users and, in doing so, process a mobile advertising identifier and related device information as described in Section 2.6. 5.2 Each processor or partner acts under its own privacy policy and applicable data-processing terms. 5.3 We may also disclose information: (a) if required to do so by law, regulation, subpoena, or other legal process; (b) to enforce our Terms of Service or protect the rights, property, or safety of the Provider, our users, or the public; (c) in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, in which case the successor entity will be bound by this Policy or will provide notice of changes; and (d) with your consent.

  1. RESET NOTIFICATIONS

If, and only if, You grant notification permission on Your device, the Application schedules two reminders locally on that device: when Your daily questions reset and when Your Faster Answers reset. The Application does not use these reminders for advertising or promotional messages. You may withdraw permission at any time in Your device settings; the Application then cancels its pending reset reminders.

  1. DATA RETENTION

7.1 Images you photograph are processed to generate an answer and are not written to our persistent server storage. For signed-out users, question content is processed transiently. If you sign in, limited text history and answers you choose to save are stored with your account so they can sync across devices; you can clear that history or delete the account in the App. 7.2 Usage counters associate the anonymous device identifier with dates and counts and are retained as needed to enforce limits and prevent abuse. 7.3 Subscription and referral entitlement records are retained as needed to honor access, prevent repeated redemption, resolve billing issues, and meet legal obligations. 7.4 Support and feedback messages are retained as reasonably necessary to address inquiries and maintain business records. 7.5 Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is retained under Google's policies. 7.6 We may retain information longer where required by law, or in anonymized or aggregated form that does not identify you.

  1. DATA SECURITY

8.1 All traffic between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. 8.2 API credentials and server secrets are stored server-side and are never embedded in the App or distributed to devices. 8.3 We employ commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. 8.4 No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You use the App at your own risk to the extent permitted by applicable law.
