Subscription & billing

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, not by us, so they are cancelled in your store settings:

  • iPhone or iPad: Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Solve My Homework AI → Cancel Subscription
  • Android: Play Store → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Solve My Homework AI → Cancel

You can also reach the same place from Profile → Manage Subscription in the app.

164

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. It has to be cancelled in the store, or billing continues.

How does the 3-day free trial work, and when am I charged?

The free trial is a one-time welcome offer on the yearly plan, shown when you first install the app. You get full PRO access for three days at no cost.

If you do not cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the yearly subscription starts automatically and your store account is charged. Cancel any time during the trial and you pay nothing, keeping PRO until the trial runs out.

The weekly plan has no trial — it bills straight away.

I paid but I do not have PRO. What now?

First try Restore Purchases. Open Profile → Manage Subscription and it is at the top right of that screen. It re-links a subscription bought on this or another device, and fixes it in almost every case — particularly after reinstalling the app or moving to a new phone.

Restoring only works in the installed app, and you must be signed in to the same Apple or Google account that made the purchase.

If PRO still does not appear, email us with the store receipt Apple or Google sent you and we will sort it out.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled entirely by the store that took the payment — we have no ability to issue one ourselves.

  • Apple: go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account
  • Google: go to play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions, or request it through Google Play support

If a charge looks wrong to you, email us anyway — we will look at what happened and tell you honestly what we find.

What do I actually get with PRO?

Unlimited questions and snaps with no daily limit, our smartest AI model, unlimited faster answers, and no ads or interruptions.

The free version stays free: a set number of AI questions every day, supported by ads. You never have to pay to use the app.

Your account & your data

Do I need an account?

No. Every core feature — photo solving, typed questions, reading, translation, the calculator and chat history — works without signing in.

An account is optional and gives you one thing: your chats, history and saved answers live on the account instead of on one phone, so they follow you to a new device and survive a reinstall.

How do I sign in?

Open Profile → Log In / Sign Up and choose Continue with Apple or Continue with Google. Those are the only two sign-in methods, and both are handled by Apple and Google themselves — we never see or store a password.

How do I delete my account and everything in it?

In the app, it takes about ten seconds and you do not need to contact us:

  1. Tap Profile (bottom right)
  2. Tap your account at the top to open My Account
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account
  4. Type DELETE to confirm

Deletion is immediate and permanent, and removes your email address, name, profile, history and saved answers from our servers.

If you no longer have the app installed, the same thing can be done from this page.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription — that is billed by Apple or Google and has to be cancelled in the store.

What do you do with my questions and photos?

They are sent over an encrypted connection to our server and to the AI services that generate your answer, and that is what they are used for. We do not use them for advertising and we do not build advertising profiles.

The full detail is in our Privacy Policy.

Using the app

The answer I got looks wrong.

It happens — AI gets things wrong, and we would genuinely like to know when it does. Send us the question and what you got back, and it helps us improve the model prompts for everyone.

Two things that improve accuracy a lot: crop to the complete question including any diagram, and make sure the whole thing sits inside the white frame before you confirm.

The camera is black, or the flash will not fire.

Check that the app is allowed to use the camera — Settings → Solve My Homework AI → Camera on iPhone and iPad, or Settings → Apps → Solve My Homework AI → Permissions on Android.

If the permission is on and the screen is still black, close the app fully and reopen it. If it keeps happening, email us with your device model and OS version — that one detail usually tells us exactly what is going on.

I have hit my daily limit.

The free tier includes a set number of questions each day. The counter resets automatically — the app tells you the time it comes back.

You can also turn on notifications in Settings and the app will tell you when your questions reset. PRO removes the limit entirely.

The app says it cannot reach the server.

The app needs an internet connection to answer a question, because the AI runs on our servers rather than on your phone. Check your connection and try again.

If you are connected and it still fails, it may be us — email us and we will check whether something is down at our end.

How does Refer & Earn work?

Share your code from Profile → Refer & Earn. Your friend installs the app and uses it for seven days, then pastes your code in Profile → Got a Referral Code?. You each get a free week of PRO.

A code can be used once per device, and rewards are capped at 12 weeks. The seven-day wait is there to keep the rewards going to real students.
